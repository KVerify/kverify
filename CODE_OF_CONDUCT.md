# Code of Conduct

KVerify is a small open source project. The goal is to keep discussion useful, respectful, and focused on making the
library better.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Critique ideas, APIs, code, and tradeoffs, not people.
- Assume good intent where reasonable.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Help make the project approachable for people with different levels of Kotlin experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized comments, threats, and publishing private information
are not acceptable.

## Maintainer action

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that make the
project harder to participate in.

Serious or repeated violations may result in being blocked from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you need to report a problem, contact the maintainer at real.tessoir@gmail.com.

Reports will be handled with care and kept private where possible.
